# Special Collections

During the course of its life cycle, the FAAM project has explored the knowledge potential hidden behind handwritten and editorial annotations in musical scores within a variety of contexts, ranging from the analysis of Early Music editions to performance indication made by soloists, composers and conductors.

The listed collections may provide new research insights for future projects in musicology and artistic research of the _long 19th century_.

## Early Music movement

The history of the Early Music movement and its origins is a fascinating subject deserving more research from both academic and artistic disciplines. 

FAAM provides an extensive corpus of editions and period compositions, i.e. musical pieces inspired by older traditions of the Renaissance and Baroque. The [Missa Romantica](https://vecchiemusiche.be/projects/missa_romantica/) and[Echos du temps passé](https://github.com/nicholascornia89/echos_du_temps_passe) projects have risen from this special collection, the first investigating the sacred polyphony of the so-called [Cecilian Movement](https://nicholascornia89.github.io/faam/entity/ZMpsLcpT), the latter focusing on the comparative analysis of several editions of [Domenico Scarlatti](https://nicholascornia89.github.io/faam/entity/PXYCpRXf) and [Johann Sebastian Bach](https://nicholascornia89.github.io/faam/entity/GecAsFfL) keyboard music.

We have focused our attention to editions with a clear intention as a performing tool, rather than a scholarly product. Controversial editors of Early Music such as [Robert Franz](https://nicholascornia89.github.io/faam/entity/T4UPmCxj) and [Franz Xaver Witt](https://nicholascornia89.github.io/faam/entity/UJq8vYVd) have been included alongside scholars more in line with modern historically informed practice aesthetics.

## Salon music

The realm of amateur and domestic music making is also a neglected field of investigation. In the last decades, more academic and artistic attention has been dedicated to [salon music](https://nicholascornia89.github.io/faam/entity/mGvrzsgB), played outside the great theatres and concert halls [^november].

Chamber music [arrangements](https://nicholascornia89.github.io/faam/entity/WiBZjkbR)  and adaptations for [keyboard four hands](https://nicholascornia89.github.io/faam/entity/fygECL6d) and other domestic settings provide additional insights on the performance practice of the time, because the editor had to provide additional guidance to their amateur consumers. 

Music periodicals are also playing a crucial role in the understanding of domestic performance practice, especially by female players. Here is a list of interesting series:

- [L'Illustration](https://nicholascornia89.github.io/faam/entity/9JZUardt) French periodical with music
- [Journal des Demoiselles](https://nicholascornia89.github.io/faam/entity/Asu9TycB)
- [Le trésor des demoiselles](https://nicholascornia89.github.io/faam/entity/jJVJWFa2)
- [Album Musica](https://nicholascornia89.github.io/faam/entity/UKFh22Sf)

Fellow researcher Pauline Lebbe has started a new artistic research project on the topic called [_Echoes of Home_](https://ap-arts.be/en/research/echoes-home-reimagining-domestic-musical-soundscape-19th-century-belgium), focusing on Belgian salon music.

[^november]: November, Nancy. 2023. _The Age of Musical Arrangements in Europe: 1780–1830_. Elements in Music and Musicians 1750–1850. Cambridge University Press.

## Orchestral music

The FAAM corpus contains several [orchestral scores](https://nicholascornia89.github.io/faam/entity/fTDkNCW3) by famous conductors, such as Mahler pioneer [Maurice Abravanel](https://nicholascornia89.github.io/faam/entity/MjskrVov), but also music that testify the performance of Belgian ensembles, such as the annotated Haydn [symphony](https://nicholascornia89.github.io/faam/entity/Zgsxtx6S) by Ghent conservatory dean [Karel Miry](https://nicholascornia89.github.io/faam/entity/NwoWGVoL) or the Liszt [piano concerto](https://nicholascornia89.github.io/faam/entity/iHeydB2F) performed by [Hélène Dinsard](https://nicholascornia89.github.io/faam/entity/GgyGnGfG).


## Concert programmes

Alongside handwritten annotations, information recorded in [concert booklets](https://nicholascornia89.github.io/faam/entity/jSGDbTZp) and exam programmes have played a marginal role in musicology and artistic research. We have collected a series of concert programmes from the Royal Conservatoires of [Ghent](https://nicholascornia89.github.io/faam/entity/KW3RZcDp) (1835-1878) and [Antwerp](https://nicholascornia89.github.io/faam/entity/SGA8i9nK) (1899-1912) reconstructing the intricate networks of artistic legacy between students and teachers, and analysing the evolution of repertoire in music education. Furthermore, the concert booklets of the [Royal Society for Zoology Antwerp](https://nicholascornia89.github.io/faam/entity/GK7UXWTC) (1896-1905) provides us with a detailed overview of the activity of one of Antwerp's most important orchestra, under the baton of [Edward Keurvels](https://nicholascornia89.github.io/faam/entity/2gUssDxo).

## Annotation types

Music notation is one of the most complex languages ever designed by human beings. It is no surprise to state that the classification and understanding of annotations has been a challenge not yet fully solved [^a]. 

[^a]: Cohen-Shalit, Nir. 2025. “The Romantic Conductor-Scholar.” In _Performing by the Book? Musical Negotiations between Text and Act_. Orpheus Institute Series. Leuven University Press.

According to the type of annotations arising from the corpus, we have constructed a simple [annotation ontology](./music_annotation_ontology), mostly based on [Wikidata](https://www.wikidata.org/) entities.
