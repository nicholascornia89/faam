# score complexity

!!! info "Description"
	Complexity level of a manifestation according to its layout and impression technique.
	- [Mechanical print](https://nicholascornia89.github.io/faam/entity/WJHHh56W): This engraving format is the most common for the FAAM corpus, where scores were engraved mechanically before the advent of digital editions.
	- [Lithographic quality](https://nicholascornia89.github.io/faam/entity/cmQGFaLi): Lithography is an old technique allowing to reproduce handwritten documents, usually created by professional artists. Also, rehearsal score were usually made by professional copyists, with a regular and clear handwriting.
	- [Manuscript sketch](https://nicholascornia89.github.io/faam/entity/KxvTqqX5):  In the last category falls all the other types of handwritten scores made by composers and musicians, usually lacking regularity in the layout.
